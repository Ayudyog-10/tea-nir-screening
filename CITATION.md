METASPEQ (2026). *Can NIR predict a tea taster? An audit of three MetaspeQ tea campaigns, 2023–2026.*
Ayudyog Private Limited. Data: Tocklai Tea Research Institute (Aug 2023, Jan 2026), Dhunsree Tea (Jun 2023).

If any of this is written up for publication, the honest framing is a negative
result with positive controls: the instrument resolves batch and physical form,
while tea-taster scores are not predictable from the dry-leaf spectra at these
sample sizes and reference resolutions.
